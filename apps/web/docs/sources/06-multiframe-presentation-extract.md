## Problem framing (no price)
- Growth of floors / density; health & psychological discomfort from noise
- Hard-surface interiors (minimalism, loft) worsen acoustics
- Stretch-ceiling air gap creates «эффект барабана»; MultiFrame dissipates air energy via perforation

## Product tech
- Rigid energy-absorbing molded frame — AEROCELL TECHNOLOGY™
- Fillers: Biplast Eco Foam (elastic) / PPU Full Touch (dense)
- Through acoustic perforation; slot perforations 1–4% covering 100–500 Hz resonance band
- SmartLock: 5 mounting holes; tongue-and-groove continuous surface; cut-to-size
- Size 0.75×0.55×0.06 m; ~1 kg/panel; pack of 4; up to ~17 dB (vendor claim)
- Mount on ceiling or walls; any slab type; communications before or after panels

## Flat vs Wave
- Wave: Eco Foam; wavy face; can stay as visible industrial-design finish
- Flat: PPU Full Touch; flat face; convenient for fixtures/lighting under finish
